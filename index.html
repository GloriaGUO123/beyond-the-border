Every Child Deserves the Opportunity to Thrive

Supporting newly arrived children and cross-border students in Hong Kong through accessible resources, evidence-based research, and policy evaluation.

项目的使命与目标 (Mission & Vision)

Every child deserves access to education, support, and opportunities to reach their full potential, regardless of their background or circumstances. Beyond the Border is an independent initiative dedicated to supporting newly arrived children and cross-border students in Hong Kong.

1. Educational Access

Navigating school admissions, understanding local curricula, and accessing appropriate learning support tailored to individual eligibility.

2. Language & Belonging

Addressing language barriers (Cantonese & English), adjusting to social expectations, and building peer relationships.

3. Access to Information

Streamlining scattered service information for families and addressing commuting and scheduling challenges.

Policy Analysis & Evaluation

Evaluating existing Hong Kong SAR support framework and identifying policy improvement opportunities.

5 Key Policy Recommendations

  • Recommendation 01: Centralized Support Information Hub
  • Recommendation 02: Strengthen Follow-up Support After Adaptation
  • Recommendation 03: Flexible Extracurricular Scheduling for Commuters
  • Recommendation 04: Improve Coordination Between Schools and Family Services
  • Recommendation 05: Increase Transparency in Policy Outcome Data
